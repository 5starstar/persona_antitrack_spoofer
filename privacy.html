Persona Shield (BETA) – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Persona Shield (BETA) is a Chrome extension that gives each browser profile one consistent, randomly generated device identity, to resist cross-site fingerprint tracking. This extension is for beta testing.

What we collect

Nothing. The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, page content, or any other user data. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests of its own.

What is stored on your device

The extension saves two things in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local): the randomly generated device identity it presents to websites, and your on/off settings. This data stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

Why the extension needs access to all websites

Fingerprinting can happen on any site, so the extension runs on all sites in order to replace device details (such as platform, CPU core count, and graphics information) that pages can read. It does not read, record, or store the content of the pages you visit or the addresses you visit. It also modifies the User-Agent and related Client Hints headers on outgoing requests so they match the identity shown to pages. It does not log or store those requests.

Third parties

None. The extension loads no remote code and contacts no third-party services.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
